# How period life expectancy can distort our interpretation of mortality crises

This repository contains materials to reproduce the results of the manuscript:

Miranda, M.L., Basellini, U., Acosta, E., Rau, R. & Zagheni, E. How period life expectancy can distort our interpretation of mortality crises.


### Structure

- dat : data
- fun : functions
- out : output figures and summary tables
- src : code to replicate analysis and visualisation


#### All data employed in this study are publicly available and can be found at:

World Population Prospects 2024 Revision (2024). United Nations, Department of Economics and Social Affairs, Population Division. Available at https://population.un.org/wpp/.

Human Mortality Database (2025). University of California, Berkeley (USA) and Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research (Germany). Available at www.mortality.org or www.humanmortality.de.
